# Brian Blaze

Senior Developer based in the West Midlands, UK. Full-stack engineer with 8+ years building production systems — primarily Java/Spring Boot and Python — for enterprise and mortgage lending platforms.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brianblaze) · [X](https://x.com/blaze__b__)

---

## What I work on

At **Phoebus Software** (Solihull, UK — since Aug 2023), I lead a team on **Phoebus 1 & 2**, a desktop and web suite for the mortgage lending industry. Recent focus areas include:

- Championing trunk-based development across the team
- Designing Swagger/OpenAPI specs for external mortgage service integrations
- Migrating the communications framework to Spring Boot and integrating Quadient Inspire letter generation

Previously, as Senior Product Engineer at **LTIMindtree** (Bangalore, Dec 2020 – Jul 2023), I worked on **Lumin AI**, a virtual analyst tool, and led a team of 5+ developers.
